Privacy Policy

Effective August 5, 2026 · Diction - Offline Dictionary · Yaas Albudoory

TLDR

Diction does not collect, store, or share any personal data. No accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

Dictionary lookups are offline

Definitions come from a dictionary database bundled inside the app (built from WordNet 3.1). Looking up a word never contacts a server and works fully in airplane mode. Your searches are never sent anywhere.

Data stored on your device

Your saved words and your last-searched word are stored locally on your device (and, for the last-searched word, in a private app group so the Home Screen widget can display it). This data never leaves your device and is removed when you delete the app.

When the app uses the network

Diction only uses the network in two optional, user-initiated cases. Neither one collects personal information:

  1. Optional dictionary-pack downloads. If you choose to download an additional dictionary from the in-app list, the app fetches that dictionary file. No personal data is sent with the request.
  2. The optional tip. If you choose to leave a one-time tip from Settings → Support, the transaction is handled entirely by Apple's App Store (StoreKit). Diction never sees or stores your payment details. Apple's handling of that purchase is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

The tip is optional, unlocks nothing, and is the app's only purchase.

No third-party sharing

Diction contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. Because no personal data is collected, none is shared or sold.

Children's privacy

Diction is safe for all ages. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new effective date.
