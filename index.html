dic·tion

/ˈdik·shən/ · noun

  1. a calm, offline dictionary for iPhone. Every definition is built in, and lookups never touch the network.
  2. a Home Screen widget that keeps your last-searched word close, set in a quiet serif.
  3. one word on one card: swipe right to keep it, left to let it go.

[for iPhone, iOS 26 or later; free, with no account and no ads. Definitions are based on WordNet 3.1.]

Common questions

Does Diction need an internet connection?
No. The dictionary is bundled inside the app, so search works anywhere, even in airplane mode. The network is only used if you choose to download an optional dictionary pack.
Where are my saved words kept?
On your iPhone. There is no account and no cloud. Deleting the app deletes them.
How do I add the widget?
Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen, tap the Add button, and search for Diction. Tapping the widget opens the app straight into search.
What does the tip in Settings do?
Nothing, on purpose. It unlocks no features. It's a one-time way to say thanks, handled entirely by the App Store.
