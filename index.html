dic·tion

/ˈdik·shən/ · noun

  1. a calm, offline dictionary for iPhone. Every definition is built in — lookups work in airplane mode and never touch the network.
  2. a Home Screen widget that keeps your last-searched word close, set in a quiet serif.
  3. one word on one card: swipe right to keep it, left to let it go.

[for iPhone, iOS 26 or later; free, with no account and no ads. Definitions are based on WordNet 3.1.]

Common questions

Does Diction need an internet connection?
No. Definitions come from a dictionary bundled inside the app, so search works anywhere — including airplane mode. The only time Diction uses the network is if you choose to download an optional dictionary pack.
Where are my saved words kept?
On your iPhone. There is no account and no cloud — nothing you do in Diction leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes your saved words with it.
How do I add the widget?
Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen, tap the Add button, and search for Diction. The widget shows your last-searched word; tapping it opens the app straight into search.
What does the tip in Settings do?
Nothing — on purpose. It unlocks no features and removes no limits. It's a single, optional way to say thanks, handled entirely by the App Store.
